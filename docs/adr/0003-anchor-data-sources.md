# Anchor Responses come from commercially licensed human-scored corpora

We dropped the Hugging Face set `chillies/IELTS-writing-task-2-evaluation` (previously the source of the Exam Bank's IELTS Task 2 Prompts and of our calibration essays): it has no licence, its essays were web-scraped, and its per-criterion "evaluations" were generated by Gemini after being told the band (see `docs/research/data-and-licensing.md`). No public corpus offers real IELTS examiner Bands under a commercial licence, so Anchor Responses for calibration and evals come from DREsS (MIT, EFL essays, expert-scored 1–5) and ASAP 2.0 (CC BY 4.0) until we commission a small IELTS set double-marked by certified examiners. Non-commercial corpora (ELLIPSE, Cambridge FCE, W&I) stay out unless their licensors grant permission in writing.
